Free usage limit reached

Reference: this is a OmnIDE feature. OmnIDE has no plans or usage limits; see Changes.

As a developer on the Free plan, I want to know at once when I am out of agent usage and what to do, so that I am not left waiting on a turn that will never run. Click a step to open its screen; each screen links on through its real buttons. See the flow.

  1. Agent turn: error
    The next turn ends at once: "Agent error: You've hit your usage limit."
  2. Agent turn: error
    The error stays in the chat with an upgrade offer.
  3. OmnIDE Settings · Claude: error
    Settings > Claude shows the used-up Free allowance.

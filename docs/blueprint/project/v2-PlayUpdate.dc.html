A bad update is refused

As a developer, I want a bad update to be refused and retried, so that a broken download never replaces my working editor. Click a step to open its screen; each screen links on through its real buttons. See the flow.

  1. Update ready
    A toast says an update is ready and offers Restart to update.
  2. Update failed
    The checksum did not match, the download was deleted and Retry now is offered.
